## Slide 2: Motivation

**The Legibility Problem in Human-Robot Collaboration**

When a robot and human share a workspace:
- The robot must communicate its intent through motion
- **Legible motion** = observer quickly infers the goal (Dragan et al., 2013)
- Ambiguous trajectories → human waits → slower, less safe collaboration

**This work:** Can diffusion policies generate **both successful AND legible** manipulation trajectories, guided by Vision-Language Model feedback?

---

## Slide 3: Contributions

Three contributions:

1. **VLM-based legibility evaluation pipeline** — Gemini 2.5 Flash achieves 94.7% goal-prediction accuracy. Defines VLM Onset (VLO) metric.

2. **Systematic comparison of 4 training-free guidance methods** — Classifier guidance, Best-of-N, Classifier-Free Guidance, VLM reranking.

3. **End-to-end pipeline** improving early legibility from 0.898 → 0.972 (+7.4pp) at 100% task success (p = 0.00042).

## Slide 6: Diffusion Policy Architecture

**DDPM U-Net for Action Chunk Prediction**

```
obs(22) → obs_embed(256) ─────────────┐
time(t) → sinusoidal(128) → MLP(256) ─┤
noisy_actions(5) → proj(256) ──────────┘
     ↓                                ↓ (additive conditioning)
  Encoder: [256→512→1024]
  Bottleneck: [1024→1024]
  Decoder: [2048→512, 1024→256] (skip connections)
     ↓
  Output: MLP(256→5) — noise ε prediction
```

- Horizon=32, execute=8, DDIM 10-step inference
- Training: 100 epochs, lr=2e-4, EMA=0.999, batch=64
- Loss: 0.154 → 0.045

## Slide 8: VLM Evaluation Pipeline

**Gemini 2.5 Flash as Legibility Judge**

1. Render episode → MP4 video
2. Extract k=6 cumulative prefix windows (first 30% of episode)
3. VLM predicts goal: {pA, pB, cue, choice}
4. **VLO** = first window with correct prediction

**Prompt engineering was critical:**
- V1: 45% accuracy (chance level)
- V2: **97.5% accuracy** — annotated frames, image-space reasoning, temp=0.1

**Expert demo VLO ordering:** legible (2.93) < neutral (3.00) < deceptive (3.71) ✓

## Slide 9: Method 1 — Classifier Guidance

$$\hat{\epsilon}_\theta = \epsilon_\theta - w\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t} \nabla_{a_t} L_{\text{score}}$$

$L_{\text{score}}$: 4-criteria function (proximity, direction, lateral, speed). Generated by Gemini from text prompt (r=0.992 vs hand-crafted).

| Scale w | Success | L_early |
|---------|:-------:|:-------:|
| 0 | 95% | 0.906 |
| 5 | 90% | 0.946 |
| **10** | **100%** | **0.952** |
| 20 | 100% | 0.948 |

**w=10 optimal.** Note: This is classifier guidance (Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021), NOT DPS. True DPS tested → 0% success.

## Slide 10: Method 2 — Best-of-N

Sample N trajectories, pick highest L_early.

| N | L_early | Δ |
|---|:-------:|:-:|
| 1 | 0.732 | — |
| 4 | 0.779 | +6.4% |
| 8 | 0.797 | +8.8% |
| 16 | 0.806 | +10.1% |

Simple, gradient-free, diminishing returns above N=16.

## Slide 11: Method 3 — LegDiff (CFG)

$$\hat{\epsilon} = \epsilon_\theta(a_t, \emptyset) + w(\epsilon_\theta(a_t, G) - \epsilon_\theta(a_t, \emptyset))$$

Trains conditional + unconditional model. Conv1d backbone, w=3.

| Condition | Success | L_early |
|-----------|:-------:|:-------:|
| Baseline | 100% | 0.922 |
| LegDiff | 100% | 0.935 |

## Slide 18: Summary

**Thesis Statement:** Training-free guidance over diffusion policies is a viable and effective approach to legible motion generation in multi-goal manipulation.

**Evidence:**
- VLM evaluation pipeline: 94.7% accuracy, VLO metric validated
- Classifier guidance at w=10: 100% success, L_early = 0.952
- Full pipeline with VLM reranking: L_early = 0.972 (p=0.00042 vs baseline)
- 4 guidance methods compared on same task, same evaluation framework

**Honest limitations:** No human study, same configs, terminology corrections needed.
